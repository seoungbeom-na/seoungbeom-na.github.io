Economic Feasibility and Strategic Planning for Floating Solar Power Plants in Korea: A Real Options Approach

국내 수상태양광 발전사업의 경제성 평가와 전략 계획: 실물옵션 접근
Seoungbeom Na, Woosik Jang, Youngwoong Lee · DOI 10.3390/su17010137

A 100 MW floating solar plant on idle water surface is already profitable on paper. If the site could later be expanded to 200 MW, how much is that possibility worth, and how should it change the plan?

02What it means

The expansion option is worth substantially more than the base project itself, so the decision that matters is not whether to build but how to stage capacity.
