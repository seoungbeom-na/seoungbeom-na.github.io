Real Options-Based Feasibility Evaluation of Offshore Wind Farm Development in Korea's Idle Coastal Areas

유휴 연안을 활용한 해상풍력 개발의 실물옵션 기반 타당성 평가
Seoungbeom Na, Jaebin Lee, Woosik Jang · DOI 10.3390/en18184976

The Geumil offshore wind farm in Wando is not viable on a conventional discounted cash flow basis. Does the ability to expand or contract capacity later change that verdict, and what policy setting would make the project stand on its own?

01Key figures and tables

FIG 01

Study site

The 600 MW farm is placed in an idle coastal zone off Geumil, Wando, and connected to the Wando substation. Siting on idle water avoids the land-use conflict that constrains onshore capacity.

FIG 02

Price paths from Monte Carlo simulation

Eleven years of KPX price records are fitted to a Geometric Brownian Motion model, and 1,000 simulated paths generate the twenty-year price range. This yields the volatility input of 23.04% used in the option valuation.

Contracting is better than maintaining the current scale, and expanding is better than contracting. The ranking, not the absolute values, is what guides the operating strategy.

FIG 04

Where policy turns the project around

At the current REC weight of 2.71 the project stays unprofitable; it turns positive above 3.01. The chart divides the decision space into a contraction zone below 1.5, a neutral zone from 1.5 to 3.0, and an expansion zone above 3.01.

02What it means

The options add strategic value but do not rescue the project on their own. Viability is set by the REC weight, which makes the certificate scheme the decisive variable rather than the engineering design.
