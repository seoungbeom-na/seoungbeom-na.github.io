Robustness Maps for Hydrogen Delivery Mode Selection Under Construction Cost Uncertainty: Application to Korean Hydrogen Corridors

건설비 불확실성 하의 수소 수송방식 선택을 위한 강건성 지도: 한국 수소 회랑 적용
Seoungbeom Na, Woosik Jang, Chang-Geun Lee · DOI 10.3390/en19174111

Whether hydrogen should be delivered by pipeline or by truck is a large and largely irreversible investment decision, yet the cost information behind it varies by a factor of several across the literature. From which combinations of distance and demand does the choice remain unchanged however costs are realized?

01Key figures and tables

FIG 01

Analytical framework in four stages

Mode-specific work breakdown structures are coupled with one common risk breakdown structure, Monte Carlo simulation draws 20,000 cost realizations at every grid condition, the synthesized distributions are examined against Korean records, and the result is summarized as a robustness map.

FIG 02

Robustness maps of the least-cost delivery mode

Where a convertible natural gas line exists, repurposing satisfies the 80% win rate criterion in 162 of 196 conditions and no condition makes a new pipeline robust. In a new corridor the new pipeline dominates 125 conditions and the tube trailer 48, separated by a gray band of 21 conditions in which assumptions decide the outcome.

FIG 03

Effect of the liquefaction cost attribution boundary

The two panels differ in exactly one assumption, the inclusion or exclusion of the liquefaction plant investment and its electricity. Excluding them raises the share of the map on which the LH2 tanker truck is robust from 1% to 72.4%, so the viability of liquefied delivery is decided by contract structure rather than by technology.

The repurposing-first conclusion survives a distinct retrofit risk profile. Even at a mean overrun of 3.25 times the new-build mean, repurposing still holds 127 conditions, and beyond 3.5 times its robust region is absorbed into the assumption-dominated band without passing to the new pipeline.

FIG 05

Which input uncertainty moves the threshold most

Each factor was fixed in turn at its 10th and 90th percentiles while the others retained their distributions. Construction cost overrun dominates and moves the threshold between 12,700 and 25,100 t/yr, followed by the 8 inch unit cost and the pipeline O&M rate, so the two leading factors are both matters of what pipeline construction actually costs.

FIG 06

Migration of the threshold under four condition sets

Raising the O&M rate from 1% to 5% per year lifts the threshold by 45.8%, extending the planning horizon from 10 to 30 years lowers it by 38.3%, and a ten-year demand ramp raises it by 23.2%. Raising the carbon price path to 13.8 times the reference moves it by only 2.2%.

Apart from a deliberately coarsened two-tier diameter ladder, the influence of the methodological choices stays between -4.5% and +12.8%. These effects are one order of magnitude smaller than the condition effects, which supports the reading that the conclusions derive from the data rather than from the method.

FIG 08

Announced Korean plans placed on the map

Four announced Korean hydrogen delivery plans are placed with coordinates estimated from public announcements and anonymized. The two pilot-scale projects sit in the tube trailer region and the two large projects in the robust pipeline region, which is consistent with their project stages and indicates that the map can serve as a planning-stage diagnostic tool.

02What it means

Screening for a convertible existing line should precede any review of new pipeline construction, because no condition within the analyzed range made a new line robust where repurposing was available. Where no such line exists, a new pipeline becomes robustly justified from approximately 19,191 t/yr at 150 km, and the levers that advance that transition are measured O&M records, longer appraisal horizons, and early demand commitment rather than the carbon price.
