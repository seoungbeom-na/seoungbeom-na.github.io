Modeling the Causal Relationships of Country Risk Factors Using Expert Experience and Intuition-Based Sensing Data

전문가 경험·직관 기반 센싱 데이터를 활용한 국가 리스크 요인의 인과관계 모형화
Seoungbeom Na, Chang Geun Lee, Woosik Jang, Inju Hwang, Hongcheol Lee · DOI 10.18494/SAM5596

Cost data from international construction projects is confidential, so direct measurement is rarely possible. Can expert judgment be treated as sensor-like data to identify which country risk factors actually drive cost overrun, and through which paths?

PathStandardized βType

The environmental path is the largest at 0.360 and is significant at p below 0.001, while the legal path is the smallest at 0.067. Legal risk therefore matters far less to cost than conventional checklists assume.

Environmental and market constructs are well explained by the model, which supports using it to prioritise where risk management effort is spent.

02What it means

Because environmental risk carries the largest direct effect and culture feeds into it, management attention is better spent on environmental and cultural exposure than on the legal factors that dominate conventional checklists.
