About

Research overview

Seoungbeom Na is an Assistant Professor of Smart Architecture and Civil Engineering at Hanyeong University. Grounded in construction management and engineering economics, his research addresses a central question of infrastructure development: how large projects should be valued, and how their risks should be quantified and managed, under market and policy uncertainty.

His work rests on three methodological pillars: economic feasibility assessment, quantitative risk analysis, and stochastic simulation for quantifying uncertainty. These extend across a broad range of applications, from renewable energy and hydrogen infrastructure to unconventional oil and gas development and international construction. In parallel, he studies smart construction, drawing on field data from drone- and LiDAR-based spatial mapping. He received his Ph.D. from Chosun University in 2025.

Research

Research areas

Grounded in construction management, his research spans valuation, risk, and simulation, from building and infrastructure projects to renewable energy, hydrogen, and unconventional oil and gas development.

⚡

Economic Feasibility Assessment

Valuation and investment decision-making for energy projects, from renewable energy and energy plants to unconventional oil and gas resources, combining real options analysis with cash-flow based appraisal.

📊

Quantitative Risk Analysis

Identification, quantification, and causal modeling of project risks, from construction cost volatility to country risk in international projects.

🎲

Uncertainty Quantification & Simulation

Financial-engineering approaches to uncertainty, applying stochastic price processes and Monte Carlo simulation to forecasting and decision support.

🛰️

Smart Construction

Drone- and LiDAR-based 3D mapping, airborne bathymetric LiDAR, and digital transformation of infrastructure and the water industry.
