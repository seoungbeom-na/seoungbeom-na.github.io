Real Options Analysis for Land and Water Solar Deployment in Idle Areas of Agricultural Dam: A Case Study of South Korea

농업용 댐 유휴부지의 육상·수상 태양광 개발에 대한 실물옵션 분석
Seoungbeom Na, Kyeongseok Kim, Woosik Jang, Changgeun Lee · DOI 10.3390/su14042297

Idle slopes and water surfaces at agricultural dams show a negative net present value, so they are normally screened out. Is that screening decision correct once managerial flexibility is taken into account?

On cash flow alone the site is rejected. Once the right to abandon the project is priced, the same site returns a positive value, which is why idle public land and water deserve a second screening.

FIG 02

Binomial lattice for the case project

Each node carries three figures: investment cost on top, underlying asset value in the middle, and option value at the bottom. The lattice runs across the construction and early operation period of the 1 MW land plus 35 MW floating array.

FIG 03

Which assumptions matter most

Volatility is varied from 20 to 70% and the four key uncertainties by plus or minus 50%. Electricity selling price moves option value the most, followed by investment cost, maintenance cost, and inflation.

02What it means

Pricing the right to walk away turns a rejected site into a candidate, which matters because public land and water surfaces are the least contested locations for new capacity.
